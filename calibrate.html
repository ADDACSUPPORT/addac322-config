Module output calibration

Release v0.3.1

You need a multimeter on DC volts that reads to the millivolt.
  1. Power the module in the rack and connect its UART USB port to this computer (the one the serial monitor uses, not the native USB port).
  2. On the joystick, set the output range you will use most. While you measure one jack, the other three sit where the sticks rest in that range.
  3. Unpatch the outputs: a jack will be held at ±7.5 V.
Not connected.

How it works

For each jack the module outputs two test voltages, nominally −7.5 V and +7.5 V, without any correction. From what your meter reads at each, it works out that jack's zero error and scale error, and corrects every value it outputs from then on, in every range. Use Check to confirm a jack after storing it.

Nothing is kept until you press Save to the module. The outputs go back to the joystick when you press Release the outputs, or by themselves 10 minutes after the last command.
